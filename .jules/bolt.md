## 2024-05-24 - [Optimize Read-Heavy Mongoose Queries with .lean()]
**Learning:** Using `.lean()` on Mongoose `.find()` queries for read-only operations significantly improves performance by bypassing document hydration, and eliminates the need for subsequent `.toObject()` calls or mapping over arrays to convert them to plain objects. This is a crucial optimization for endpoints that fetch and return lists of documents (like doctors, resources) without needing Mongoose virtuals or instance methods.
**Action:** Always use `.lean()` for read-heavy GET endpoints where Mongoose document instance methods and virtuals are not required. Ensure redundant `.toObject()` conversions are removed when `.lean()` is applied to avoid unnecessary overhead and potential runtime errors on plain objects.
